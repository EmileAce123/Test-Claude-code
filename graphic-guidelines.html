10 — Do & Don't

✓ Do
  • Use deep dark backgrounds (#080B14 – #131728) for all surfaces
  • Apply the Purple → Pink gradient for primary CTAs and highlights
  • Use pill-shaped buttons and chips (border-radius: 9999px)
  • Maintain subtle borders (rgba white, 4–12% opacity) on all cards
  • Prefer radial or ambient glows for hero backgrounds
  • Use Inter or system-ui; keep heading letter-spacing negative
  • Layer glass/overlay panels with backdrop-filter: blur()
  • Keep animations under 300ms for interactive elements
✗ Don't
  • Use light backgrounds or white surfaces
  • Mix warm/orange tones — palette is strictly cool (blue/violet/pink)
  • Use sharp 0px corners on cards or buttons
  • Apply heavy drop shadows without a dark semi-transparent layer
  • Use solid opaque overlays — always keep glass/translucency
  • Set body text above 16px or secondary text brighter than #9BA3C4
  • Animate with linear easing — always use ease-out or spring
  • Overcrowd layouts — generous whitespace (min 24px gutters)

11 — Layout Principles

⊞
Card Grid
auto-fill / minmax(280px, 1fr) — cards breathe at equal heights with consistent gaps (20–24px).
◧
Sidebar + Main
Fixed sidebar 220–280px. Main content fills remaining width. Sidebar uses darker bg (#0E1120).
⬆
Hero Section
Centered content, full-bleed radial glow, max-width 640px for text, 2 CTAs side-by-side.
≡
Top Navigation
Glass bar, blur(16px), sticky, height 56–64px. Logo left · actions center/right · avatar right.
